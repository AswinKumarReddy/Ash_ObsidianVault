Onions, 200g

Mint leaves 20 g 

Chilies hot 100 grams or low spice 200 g 

Coriander 100 grams 

Tomato 150 g

Ginger garlic paste, 50 g 

Grated dry coconut 30 g

  

Chicken 1 KG 

  

Process

  

1. Chicken pre prep

Start with one spoon of oil and cooker 

Add half spoon of ginger, garlic paste 

Add half spoon turmeric to the oil 

Add chicken

Add one spoon salt 

Mix well for two minutes

Add quarter to half glass of water

Cook for 3 to 4 whistles 

  

1. Masala (in mixer)

Cloves 5

Black pepper half table spoon

Cinnamon 2 

Grind

Add Coconut

Grind

Add coriander powder 3 tablespoons

Grind

Onion

Tomato

Corriander

Pudina

Chilli

Grind

  

1. Cooking

Oil 2 sambar spoons

Jeera

Mustard

Curry leaves

Red chilli

Ginger garlic

Saute till ginger garlic is ccoked - 2mins

Add onions

Saute - 2mins

Add masala from step 2

Saute for 5 mins

Add chicken and salt (and water as required)

Mix well and let it cook with lid on - 20 mins

Ocassionly stir to avoid burning in bottom

Add a pinch of garam masala in the end (1min) 

Finish with a garnish of corriander
