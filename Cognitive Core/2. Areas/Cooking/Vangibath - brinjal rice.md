
Prerequicites:
- Binjal - 250gm
- Vangibat masala (packet)
- Onion - 2 onions
- Kaju 15 gm - optional
- Toasted peanuts
- Tamarind juice
  - one lemon sized tamarind crushed in water for 10mins to get the soaked juice 
- Dry or Fresh coconut
  - dry to store for later 
  - Fresh to eat immediately 
- popu stuff
  - Mustard
  - Jeera
  - Uddibyallu / uraddal
  - Senaga byallu/ chana dal
  - Red chilli
  - Curry leaves
- cooked rice


Preparation 
- start with popu
- Mustard , pulses , jeera, red chilli, curry leaves - in this order
- Next onions in (stirr till slight color changes)
- 1/4 spoon of ginger garlic paste (stir 2mins on mid flame)
- Add pre cut brinja soaked in water( with slight salt if u want to cook faster)
- Stirr for a bit and close with lid to properly cook brinjal (5mins)
- Add msala powder and tamarind juice into the kadai (7mins till oil satrt coming out)
  - full 100gm is toomuch add maybe 60 to 70gm
- add roasted peanuts
