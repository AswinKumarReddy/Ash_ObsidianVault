# Greenwich & Greenwich Peninsula

Greenwich has **two distinct pockets** that are easy to combine or explore separately:
1. **Greenwich Peninsula / The O2** (around **North Greenwich** station — where **Cineworld O2** is)
2. **Historic Greenwich Town Centre** (around **Cutty Sark DLR** — market, park, observatory, riverside pubs)

---

## Quick Cheat Sheet
- **Best time to go:** Fri–Sun for Greenwich Market + Cutty Sark street food stalls; late afternoon for sunset from Greenwich Park.
- **Movie night pairing:** Catch a film at **Cineworld O2**, then eat at **Canteen Food Hall** (Design District, short walk from North Greenwich) or hop on the 188/129 bus (10 mins) into Historic Greenwich for pub/market food.
- **Scenic arrival:** Take the **Uber Boat (Thames Clipper)** from Central London to Greenwich Pier or North Greenwich Pier—way nicer than the Tube.

---

## Famous Spots & Landmarks
- [ ] **Royal Observatory & Prime Meridian** – Stand across the 0° longitude line and see the historic telescopes.
- [ ] **General Wolfe Statue Viewpoint (Greenwich Park)** – One of London’s best skyline views looking straight over the Queen's House toward Canary Wharf.
- [ ] **Cutty Sark** – Iconic 19th-century tea clipper dry-docked by the river (cool from outside too, or tour underneath the copper hull).
- [ ] **Old Royal Naval College & Painted Hall** – "Britain's Sistine Chapel" with breathtaking baroque ceiling art (often used as a film set).
- [ ] **Queen’s House** – Free historic royal villa with the famous **Tulip Stairs** spiral staircase and great art collection.

---

## Experiences, Cinema & Things to Do
- [ ] **Cineworld at The O2 (Superscreen / 4DX / ScreenX)** – Try a different premium format here when a big release drops.
- [ ] **IFS Cloud Cable Car** – Ride from Greenwich Peninsula (right near The O2) across the Thames to Royal Docks; amazing at sunset.
- [ ] **Up at The O2** – Guided roof-walk climb over the top of The O2 dome for 360° London views.
- [ ] **Greenwich Foot Tunnel** – Walk *underneath* the River Thames from Cutty Sark to Island Gardens for the classic postcard view looking back across the river.
- [ ] **Greenwich Market** – Browse indie crafts, vintage finds, vinyl, and antiques (covered Victorian market, great even on rainy days).
- [ ] **Thames Path Stroll (Peninsula to Town Centre)** – Walk along the river from North Greenwich past The Tide elevated walkway and Enderby Wharf into Historic Greenwich (~35–45 mins).
- [ ] **The Tide (Greenwich Peninsula)** – Elevated linear park with outdoor sculptures (including Damien Hirst pieces) and riverside views.
- [ ] **TOCA Social (The O2)** – Interactive football gaming boxes + drinks/food if going with friends.

## Food, Restaurants & Street Food (What to Order)

### Historic Greenwich & Town Centre (Sit-Down)
- [ ] **Goddard’s at Greenwich** *(King William Walk)* — *Traditional Minced Beef Pie, Mash & Parsley Liquor + Bread & Butter Pudding* (operating since 1890; ultimate old-school London comfort food; jellied/stewed eels if you're brave).
- [ ] **The Golden Chippy** *(Greenwich High Road)* — *Classic Cod or Haddock & Chips* (modest, family-run spot consistently ranked among London's very best chippies).
- [ ] **Sticks’n’Sushi** *(Greenwich High Road)* — *Yakitori grilled skewers ("sticks") + Sushi tasting platters* (sleek Japanese-Danish spot right near the park).
- [ ] **Champagne + Fromage** *(Church Street / near Market)* — *Artisan French cheese boards, baked Camembert & tartines* (cozy treat spot).

### Greenwich Market & Street Food (Grazing & Quick Bites)
- [ ] **Victus & Bibo** *(Greenwich Market)* — *Loaded lamb or halloumi wraps* (famous market stall with huge, flavour-packed wraps).
- [ ] **Spanish Paella Stall** *(Greenwich Market)* — *Freshly cooked seafood or chicken & chorizo paella* from the giant pans.
- [ ] **Dulce de Leche Churros Stall** *(Greenwich Market)* — *Warm crispy churros stuffed or drizzled with caramel*.
- [ ] **Cutty Sark Weekend Street Food Market** *(Fri–Sun, by the ship)* — *Rotating global street food trucks* (great grab-and-go by the river).

### Greenwich Peninsula & Near Cineworld O2 (Pre/Post-Movie)
- [ ] **Canteen Food Hall & Bar** *(Design District, 2 min walk from North Greenwich)* — *Global indie street food* (authentic Asian noodles, bao buns, gourmet burgers, tacos—best casual pre/post-Cineworld pick when you want variety).
- [ ] **Gaucho O2** *(Inside The O2)* — *Argentine steaks (Ancho ribeye or Bife de Lomo fillet) with chimichurri & Malbec* (great for a special-occasion dinner).
- [ ] **Peninsula Restaurant / Market Brasserie** *(InterContinental London – The O2)* — *Seasonal British/European dishes or Meridian Lounge Afternoon Tea* with floor-to-ceiling Canary Wharf river views.
- [ ] **Ardoa by Vinothec Compass** *(Greenwich Peninsula)* — *Roasted Rock Octopus & Spanish Basque tapas* paired with a huge wine selection.

### Historic Riverside Pubs & Bakeries / Cafes
- [ ] **The Cutty Sark Pub** *(Ballast Quay, east of the ship)* — *Cask ales + British pub classics* in the upstairs curved-window dining room that feels like a ship's cabin.
- [ ] **The Trafalgar Tavern** *(Park Row)* — *Sunday Roast or Beer-Battered Fish & Chips* in a grand 19th-century Thames-side pub frequented by Charles Dickens.
- [ ] **The Gipsy Moth** *(Next to Cutty Sark)* — *Pint & Sunday Roast* in a big lively beer garden right beside the ship.
- [ ] **The Sail Loft** *(Victoria Parade)* — *Relaxed river-terrace drinks & pub plates* with huge glass river views.
- [ ] **Paul Rhodes Bakery** *(King William Walk)* — *Artisan sourdough, focaccia, and legendary sausage rolls* (founded by a former Michelin-starred head chef).
- [ ] **Ole & Steen** *(Crescent Arcade, near Cutty Sark)* — *Cinnamon or Cardamom Social Swirl* + coffee before a park walk.
- [ ] **River Gardens Café** *(Thames Path, east of centre)* — *Coffee, cakes & homely brunch* right on the quiet riverside walk.

---

## Hidden Gems & Bonus Detours
- [ ] **The Fan Museum** *(Crooms Hill)* – Quirky, peaceful museum in Georgian townhouses with a lovely orangery tearoom.
- [ ] **Ranger’s House** *(Edge of Greenwich Park / Blackheath)* – Historic red-brick Georgian mansion (also known as the Bridgerton house exterior) leading onto Blackheath common.
- [ ] **St. Alfege Church** – Quiet historic church near the market with a peaceful courtyard.
- [ ] **Eltham Palace** *(Short bus/train detour)* – Amazing Art Deco mansion built onto a medieval royal palace.
