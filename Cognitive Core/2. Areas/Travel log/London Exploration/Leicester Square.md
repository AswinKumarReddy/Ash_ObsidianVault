# Leicester Square, Chinatown & West End Edges

Because Leicester Square sits right where **Chinatown, Soho, and Covent Garden** meet, it's one of the best food hubs in London—as long as you step 1–5 minutes off the main square to skip the generic tourist chains.

---

## Quick Cheat Sheet
- **Best time to go:** Pre-movie lunch (12:00–12:30 or 14:30–17:30) or late-night Chinatown eats after a movie (many spots stay open late). Peak dinner queues hit 18:30–20:30.
- **Movie night tip:** Combine **Cineworld Leicester Square** (huge main screen / IMAX) with a 2-minute walk into Chinatown or Garrick Street/Covent Garden for pre- or post-film food.
- **Cash tip:** A few old-school Chinatown spots and market/bakery windows still prefer or occasionally require cash—keep £20 handy just in case.

---

## Famous Spots & Landmarks
- [ ] **Scenes in the Square Trail** – Spot the bronze film-character statues around Leicester Square Gardens (Harry Potter, Batman, Paddington, Mr. Bean, Charlie Chaplin, Gene Kelly, etc.).
- [ ] **Chinatown Gate (Wardour Street)** & lantern-lined **Gerrard Street**.
- [ ] **Swiss Court Glockenspiel** – Quirky animated clock near the edge of Leicester Square.
- [ ] **Red-Carpet Film Premiere Crowd Watch** – Catch a premiere setup in Leicester Square Gardens if one is happening on a movie evening.
- [ ] **Trafalgar Square & National Gallery** *(4-min walk south)* – Free world-class art + iconic square views down Whitehall.

---

## Experiences, Cinema & Things to Do
- [ ] **Cineworld Leicester Square — IMAX / Superscreen** – Catch a blockbuster on one of the biggest screens in the UK.
- [ ] **Prince Charles Cinema** *(Leicester Place, 1 min off the square)* – Legendary independent repertory cinema for 35mm/70mm screenings, cult classics, double bills, and Tarantino/Ghibli seasons.
- [ ] **TKTS Booth (Leicester Square)** – Same-day discounted West End theatre tickets (worth checking if you fancy a spontaneous musical/play).
- [ ] **National Portrait Gallery** *(3-min walk)* – Newly renovated gallery right off Charing Cross Road; great for a quiet pre-movie hour.
- [ ] **Cecil Court ("Booksellers' Alley")** *(3-min walk toward Charing Cross)* – Gorgeous Victorian pedestrian lane of antique bookshops, vintage maps, and curiosities (an inspiration for Diagon Alley).
- [ ] **Chinatown Food Crawl** – Do a 3-stop grazing walk instead of one sit-down meal (e.g., *Jianbing* → *Custard Bun* → *Taiyaki or Ube Ice Cream*).
- [ ] **The Hippodrome Casino Balcony / Lola’s Bar** – Even if you don't gamble, cool historic theatre interior right on the square.

## Food, Restaurants & Street Food (What to Order)

### Chinatown Classics & Noodle Institutions (1–3 min walk)
- [ ] **Lanzhou Lamian Noodle Bar** *(Lisle St)* — *Hand-pulled beef noodle soup or dry knife-cut noodles with chilli oil* (watch the chefs pull noodles in the window; loud, fast, steamy comfort food).
- [ ] **Wong Kei** *(Wardour St)* — *Char siu & crispy roast pork belly over rice, or wonton noodle soup* (infamous London institution known for brisk, no-nonsense service, giant portions, and bargain prices).
- [ ] **Joy King Lau** *(Leicester St)* — *Traditional Dim Sum (har gow, siu mai, cheung fun, roast duck)* (multi-floor Cantonese classic, great for daytime dim sum).
- [ ] **Tao Tao Ju** *(Lisle St)* — *Siu mai, truffle dumplings & Cantonese roast meats* (sleeker, slightly more upscale dim sum option).
- [ ] **Four Seasons** *(Gerrard St / Wardour St)* — *Signature Crispy Roast Duck (half duck over rice)* (world-famous for its roast duck).

### Street Food & Viral Chinatown Bites (Great Before/After a Movie)
- [ ] **Chinese Tapas House** *(Newport Place)* — *Jianbing (crispy Chinese savoury crêpe with egg, wonton cracker, herbs & chilli)* (hole-in-the-wall legend; worth a short queue).
- [ ] **Bun House** *(Lisle St)* — *Salted Egg-Yolk Custard Bun (molten warm centre) & lamb/pork steamed baos* (also check their basement bar *Wun's* nearby in Soho).
- [ ] **Bunsik** *(Charing Cross Rd / Wardour St)* — *Half-and-half mozzarella Korean corn dog coated in crispy potato cubes* (ultimate messy movie-night snack).
- [ ] **Good Friend Chicken** *(Little Newport St)* — *Taiwanese crispy popcorn chicken* dusted with plum or chilli seasoning.

### Bakeries, Tea & Desserts (Chinatown)
- [ ] **Chinatown Bakery** *(Newport Place)* — *Freshly stamped custard Taiyaki (fish-shaped cakes), BBQ pork buns & melonpan* (look for the window queue).
- [ ] **Mamason’s Dirty Ice Cream** *(Newport Court)* — *Ube (purple yam) Bilog* (ice cream toasted inside a black charcoal pandesal milk bun) or *Ube ice cream*.
- [ ] **Bubblewrap** *(Wardour St)* — *Hong Kong egg-waffle cone loaded with gelato, mochi & fruit*.
- [ ] **HEYTEA** *(Gerrard St)* — *Roasted Brown Sugar Bobo or Mango Grapefruit Boom with salted cheese foam* (famous modern tea spot).
- [ ] **Kova Patisserie** *(Soho/Chinatown edge)* — *Matcha Mille Crêpe Cake* (light layered Japanese-French pastry).

### Sit-Down Restaurants Within 3–5 Minutes (Soho & Covent Garden Edges)
- [ ] **Bancone** *(William IV St, Covent Garden edge)* — *Silk Handkerchiefs pasta with walnut butter & confit egg yolk* (one of London's best-value fresh pasta spots; counter seating is great for solo/duo dinners).
- [ ] **Dishoom** *(Upper St. Martin’s Lane)* — *House Black Daal, Gunpowder Potatoes, Pau Bhaji, Ruby Murray, or Breakfast Bacon Naan Roll* (Bombay café legend; queue moves faster than it looks).
- [ ] **Bubala** *(Garrick St)* — *Blistered Halloumi with black seed honey, Confit Garlic Labneh & Burnt-Butter Hummus* (incredible Middle Eastern vegetarian sharing plates).
- [ ] **Xu** *(Rupert St)* — *Taro dumplings, braised pork belly, Taiwanese tea pairings* (gorgeous 1930s Taipei-inspired dining room by the BAO team).
- [ ] **YiQi** *(Lisle St)* — *Charcoal-grilled beef short ribs & Southeast Asian sharing dishes* (Michelin-Guide recommended pan-Asian dining).
- [ ] **Lao Café** *(St. Giles High St / Soho edge)* — *Laotian papaya salad, crispy rice salad & fiery curries* (bustling, authentic, punchy flavours).
- [ ] **Brother Marcus** *(Slingsby Place)* — *Fluffy "Rip & Dip" pitas, mezze & Eastern Mediterranean sharing plates*.
- [ ] **J Sheekey Atlantic Bar** *(St. Martin's Court)* — *Fish & chips, oysters or seafood platter at the bar* (iconic theatrical London seafood spot, great for walk-ins).
- [ ] **Whitcomb’s** *(Inside The Londoner Hotel, Leicester Square)* — *Mediterranean market fish & pre-theatre dining* (sleek special-occasion spot right on the square).
- [ ] **Heliot Steak House** *(Inside Hippodrome Casino)* — *Prime steaks overlooking the casino floor* (surprisingly solid value for a late-night steak right on the square).

## Hidden Gems & Bonus Detours
- [ ] **St. Martin-in-the-Fields Café in the Crypt** *(Trafalgar Square)* – Eat or grab coffee underground among historic brick vaults and tombstones; they also host evening candlelight concerts.
- [ ] **Seven Dials Market** *(Short walk into Covent Garden)* – Huge indoor food hall inside an old banana warehouse (famous for *Pick & Cheese* conveyor-belt cheese bar and global street food).
- [ ] **Neal’s Yard** *(Near Seven Dials)* – Colourful hidden courtyard with indie cafes (*26 Grains* for incredible seasonal bowls/porridge) and *Neal's Yard Dairy* nearby.
- [ ] **Bar Termini** *(Old Compton St, Soho)* – Tiny, legendary Italian espresso & Negroni bar (order a classic or rose Negroni or espresso with a cannolo).
