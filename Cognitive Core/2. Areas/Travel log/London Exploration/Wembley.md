# Wembley (Wembley Park & Ealing Road)

Wembley is **two completely different experiences** in one neighbourhood:
1. **Wembley Park** *(Wembley Park station)* — Modern entertainment district with **BOXPARK Wembley**, **London Designer Outlet** (and another **Cineworld**!), Wembley Stadium, OVO Arena, and interactive experiences.
2. **Ealing Road ("Little India")** *(Wembley Central station, ~15 min walk or short bus from Wembley Park)* — One of London’s absolute best corridors for authentic Gujarati, South Indian, Sri Lankan, and street-food chaat & sweets.

---

## Quick Cheat Sheet
- **Check the Stadium/Arena calendar first:** On match or big concert days, Wembley Park is buzzing and packed (reserve restaurants or expect queues). On **non-event days**, it’s super relaxed and easy to explore at your own pace.
- **Best combo:** Spend an afternoon doing **Wembley Park** (stadium/art trail/shopping/Cineworld LDO) or head down **Ealing Road** on an empty stomach for a thali + jalebi + dosa feast.

---

## Famous Spots & Landmarks
- [ ] **Olympic Way ("Wembley Way") & The Arch** – Walk the wide promenade straight toward Wembley Stadium's iconic arch.
- [ ] **Bobby Moore Statue & Spanish Steps** – Classic photo spot outside the stadium.
- [ ] **Wembley Park Art Trail** – Free self-guided walk spotting vibrant murals, light installations, and street art scattered across the squares.
- [ ] **Shree Sanatan Hindu Mandir** *(Ealing Road)* – Stunning intricately carved limestone and marble temple in the heart of Ealing Road (modest dress, shoes off inside).

---

## Experiences, Cinema & Things to Do
- [ ] **Wembley Stadium Tour** – Walk down the players' tunnel, sit in the manager's chair, visit the England dressing rooms, and see the Royal Box.
- [ ] **Catch a Match or Concert** – Experience a live football game at Wembley Stadium or a gig/comedy show at **OVO Arena Wembley**.
- [ ] **BOXPARK Wembley** – Not just food: check out their DJ nights, interactive quizzes, big-screen sports, shuffleboard, and ping pong.
- [ ] **Troubadour Wembley Park Theatre** – Catch a large-scale musical or touring stage show in their huge 2,000-seat theatre.
- [ ] **Sixes Social Cricket** – Hit bowling machines in batting nets with drinks and food.
- [ ] **MeetspaceVR (Zero Latency)** – Free-roam multiplayer VR arena if visiting with friends.
- [ ] **Seasonal Immersive Pop-ups** – Check what exhibition is currently running at Wembley Park (e.g., *Bubble Planet*, *Mundo Pixar*, etc.).
- [ ] **London Designer Outlet (LDO) + Cineworld** – Outlet shopping (Nike, Adidas, Levi’s, etc.) paired with a film at Cineworld LDO.

## Food, Restaurants & Street Food (What to Order)

### Ealing Road — The Legendary South Asian Food Corridor (Must-Visit!)
- [ ] **Palm Beach** *(127–133 Ealing Road)* — *Bamboo Biryani or Pothi Biryani (cooked wrapped in banana leaf), Mutton Rolls & Sri Lankan/South Indian curries* (people travel across London on weekends specifically for their biryanis).
- [ ] **Asher’s Africana** *(224 Ealing Road)* — *East African-Gujarati Mini Thali / Gujarati Thali & Cassava/Bhajias* (legendary local institution—super comforting, authentic, and incredible value).
- [ ] **Vrindavan Vatika** *(148 Ealing Road)* — *Pure Veg Gujarati Thali, giant crispy Masala Dosa & Pani Puri / Dahi Puri chaat*.
- [ ] **Ashapura Sweets & Savouries** *(Near Ealing Road)* — *Piping-hot fresh syrupy Jalebis & Fafda/Gathiya* (widely praised as some of the best fresh jalebis in London).
- [ ] **Saravanaa Bhavan / Chennai Srilalitha** *(Ealing Road area)* — *Ghee Roast Dosa, Medu Vada & Filter Coffee* (classic South Indian tiffin fix).
- [ ] **Ealing Road Paan & Chaat Stalls** — Grab a *fresh sweet paan* or *bhel puri* while walking down the street.

### BOXPARK Wembley (25+ Street Food Traders Under One Roof)
- [ ] **Smoky Boys** — *Gourmet Halal Smash/Beef Burgers, BBQ Ribs & Loaded Fries*.
- [ ] **The Athenian** — *Fluffy Greek Gyros, Souvlaki Wraps & Loaded Halloumi Fries*.
- [ ] **Nanny Bill’s** — *Slow-cooked beef croquettes & heavy-hitting smash burgers*.
- [ ] **Sides** — *Crispy fried chicken tenders, wings & signature dipping sauces*.
- [ ] **Explore rotating Boxpark traders** — Great spot to graze across 2–3 stalls (Mexican tacos, Thai, bao, wood-fired pizza) in one visit.

### Wembley Park & London Designer Outlet (Sit-Down & Bakery Treats)
- [ ] **Masalchi by Atul Kochhar** *(Wembley Park)* — *Elevated Indian street-food plates, lamb chops & creative curries* (from Michelin-starred chef Atul Kochhar).
- [ ] **Pasta Remoli** *(Wembley Park)* — *Custom fresh pasta* (pick your pasta shape like tagliatelle/ravioli/gnocchi + homemade sauce like slow-cooked beef ragu or creamy mushroom truffle).
- [ ] **Bread Ahead Bakery** *(Wembley Park)* — *Giant stuffed doughnuts (Salted Caramel, Vanilla Custard, or Crème Brûlée)* (Borough Market legend with a Wembley bakery!).
- [ ] **Las Iguanas** *(London Designer Outlet)* — *Latin American tapas sharing plates, fajitas & cocktails*.

## Hidden Gems & Bonus Detours
- [ ] **Fryent Country Park** *(Short bus/walk north)* – Rolling green hills and wildflower meadows that feel completely outside London.
- [ ] **Barham Park** *(Near Wembley Central / Sudbury)* – Quiet landscaped gardens for a post-Ealing-Road walk to walk off a heavy thali.
- [ ] **Neasden Temple (BAPS Shri Swaminarayan Mandir)** *(10–15 min bus/drive from Wembley)* – One of the grandest traditional hand-carved Hindu temples outside India, built from Italian Carrara marble and Bulgarian limestone.
