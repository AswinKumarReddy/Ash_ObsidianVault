# London Exploration Hub

A living, area-by-area menu of places, experiences, and **must-try food & dishes** across London. No speed-running—pick 1 experience + 1 food spot whenever you head to an area, then tick and drop quick notes in that area's **Visit Log**.

### Classic London Experiences
- [ ] **Thames Clipper (Uber Boat)** — Ride end-to-end (e.g., Westminster → Greenwich) at sunset
- [ ] **Sunday Roast Crawl** — Try 3–4 classic London pubs and rank your favourite roast
- [ ] **Evensong** at Westminster Abbey or St. Paul's Cathedral
- [ ] **London Market Tour** — Borough, Greenwich, Brick Lane, Maltby Street, Columbia Road, Portobello Road
- [ ] **West End Musical / Play** — Try TodayTix rush tickets for a spontaneous evening show

## How to Use & Update
1. **Before heading out:** Open the area note, pick **1 unchecked experience + 1 unchecked restaurant/dish** near where you'll be (especially before or after a Cineworld show!).
2. **On the spot:** Add any cool spot you walk past straight to `- [ ]` in that note so you don't forget it next time.
3. **After a visit (2 mins):**
   - Tick `[x]` what you did.
   - Add `⭐` next to favourites worth repeating, or `~~strikethrough~~` + note for places to skip.
   - Add 2–3 quick bullets under **Visit Log & Notes** and update `visits` / `last_visited` in frontmatter.
4. **Adding a new area:** Create a new note in `London Exploration/` using the template below.
