1.  **Basic Vehicle Maintenance** (01:34)
    *   Change a flat tire
    *   Check and change engine oil
    *   Jump-start a dead battery

2.  **Cooking** (03:05)
    *   Master 5–6 meals from scratch
    *   Learn basic knife skills and food seasoning
    *   Move away from relying on recipes or pre-packaged convenience food

3.  **First Aid & Emergency Response** (04:24)
    *   Learn basic CPR
    *   Understand how to control serious bleeding
    *   Gain hands-on training with trauma gear like tourniquets

4.  **Useful Knots** (05:24)
    *   Learn 4–5 versatile knots (e.g., bowline, taut-line hitch)
    *   Practice regularly so they become second nature for tasks like securing gear

5.  **Understand Your Finances** (06:18)
    *   Track income vs. spending
    *   Build and maintain a budget
    *   Understand credit, interest, and emergency savings

6.  **Basic Outdoor Skills** (08:48)
    *   Fire starting in various conditions
    *   Water purification methods
    *   Basic shelter building and navigation (maps/compass)

7.  **Basic Home Maintenance** (10:04)
    *   Use common hand tools
    *   Handle minor repairs like leaky faucets or drywall patches
    *   Know the location of utility shut-offs (water/power)

8.  **Physical Capability** (11:22)
    *   Focus on functional strength and mobility
    *   Maintain cardiovascular health
    *   Emphasize being fit enough to handle daily tasks and unexpected physical demands

9.  **Firearms Safety & Proficiency** (12:51)
    *   Learn safe handling and storage
    *   Determine if a firearm is loaded
    *   Seek professional training and practice regularly

10. **Gather & Prepare Food** (14:07)
    *   Learn the origins of your food (hunting, fishing, or gardening)
    *   Understand how to process and clean what you harvest
    *   Safely store and cook raw ingredients
