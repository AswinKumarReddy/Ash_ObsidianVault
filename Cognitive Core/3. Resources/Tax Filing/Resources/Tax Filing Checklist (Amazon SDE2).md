# Tax Filing Checklist - Amazon SDE2

## Documents Needed

Collect:

- Form 16 from Amazon
- AIS
- Form 26AS
- Salary slips
- Bank interest statements
- Morgan Stanley RSU statements
- RSU vesting history
- Stock transaction history
- Dividend statement, if any
- Foreign tax withheld statement, if any
- SBI TTBR exchange rate references for relevant dates

---

## Assumptions to Confirm

- [ ] Residential status is Resident and Ordinarily Resident
- [ ] Correct ITR form selected, likely ITR-2 if there is no business/profession income
- [ ] RSUs are held through Morgan Stanley
- [ ] Dividend income checked
- [ ] RSU sale transactions checked, including sell-to-cover transactions

---

## Salary Filing

- Salary income
- Bonus
- Allowances
- Employer contributions
- Profit in lieu of Salary u/s 17(3): `0`

## Amazon RSU Information Required

From Morgan Stanley, collect:

- Account opening date
- Quarterly statements
- Vesting history
- Shares vested
- Shares sold for tax withholding
- Shares held on 31-Dec-2025
- Vest date for each RSU lot
- FMV or Amazon stock price on each vest date
- Sale date, sale price, and transaction costs for any sold shares
- Dividend details, if any
- Foreign tax withheld details, if any

## Schedule FA Filing

### A2 - Foreign Custodial Account

- Status: Owner
- Financial institution: Morgan Stanley Smith Barney LLC
- Address: 1 New York Plaza, 26th Floor, New York, NY 10004, USA
- ZIP: 10004

Fill:

- Account opening date: from Morgan Stanley account details
- Peak balance: highest account value during 2025
- Closing balance: account value on 31-Dec-2025
- Gross amount paid/credited: usually `0` unless dividends or cash credits exist

### A3 - Foreign Equity Interest

- Entity: Amazon.com Inc.
- Country: United States of America
- Address: 410 Terry Avenue North, Seattle, WA 98109, United States
- ZIP: 98109
- Entity type: Listed Company
- Interest type: Equity
- Acquisition date: RSU vest date
- Initial value: shares vested x Amazon price on vest date
- Peak value: highest shares held x Amazon stock price during 2025
- Closing balance: shares held on 31-Dec-2025 x Amazon closing price on 31-Dec-2025
