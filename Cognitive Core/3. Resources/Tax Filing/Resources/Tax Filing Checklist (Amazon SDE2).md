## Schedule FA Filing

### A2 - Foreign Custodial Account

- Status: Owner
- Financial institution: Morgan Stanley Smith Barney LLC
- Address: 1 New York Plaza, 26th Floor, New York, NY 10004, USA
- ZIP: 10004

Fill:

- Account opening date: from Morgan Stanley account details
- Peak balance: highest account value during 2025
- Closing balance: account value on 31-Dec-2025
- Gross amount paid/credited: usually `0` unless dividends or cash credits exist

### A3 - Foreign Equity Interest

- Entity: Amazon.com Inc.
- Country: United States of America
- Address: 410 Terry Avenue North, Seattle, WA 98109, United States
- ZIP: 98109
- Entity type: Listed Company
- Interest type: Equity
- Acquisition date: RSU vest date
- Initial value: shares vested x Amazon price on vest date
- Peak value: highest shares held x Amazon stock price during 2025
- Closing balance: shares held on 31-Dec-2025 x Amazon closing price on 31-Dec-2025
