
Travel Duration : 4 days
Time : Late July - Dates TBD


Late July is when Meghalaya becomes almost unreal:

- **Nohkalikai Falls** is at full force.
- **Seven Sisters Falls** are spectacular.
- The hills around Shillong and Cherrapunji are incredibly green.
- Misty roads and clouds rolling through valleys create an amazing atmosphere.

---

# Ideal 4-Day Itinerary

### Day 1

- Bangalore → Guwahati morning flight
- Drive to Shillong (3 hours)
- Explore Shillong cafes and Police Bazaar.

### Day 2

- Shillong → Cherrapunji
- Nohkalikai Falls
- Seven Sisters Falls
- Mawsmai Cave
- Stay overnight in Cherrapunji.

### Day 3

Choose one:

**Adventure option**

- Double Decker Living Root Bridge trek (6-7 hours).

**Relaxed option**

- Krang Suri Falls
- Wei Sawdong Falls.

### Day 4

- Dawki and Umngot River
- Return to Guwahati for evening flight.
